How to add a VHS effect to a photo or video

  1. Open your file

    Import a photo or a clip your browser can play.

  2. Set up the tape

    Turn up the grain to roughen the image. Tracking shifts a few lines out of place.

  3. Download the result

    Photos export as PNG. Video records with its audio and the applied effects.

Frequently asked questions

Can I remove the date?

Yes. Clear the date field to remove the stamp from the preview and download.

What is the output resolution?

The aspect ratio stays the same. If the longest side exceeds 1,280 pixels, it scales down to keep the export lighter.

Is my file uploaded?

The file is processed in your browser. You don’t need an account.

How long does video export take?

It records while the clip plays, so export usually takes about as long as the clip. Your browser determines whether it saves as MP4 or WebM.
